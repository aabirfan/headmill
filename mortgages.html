Mortgage questions

Good to know

How much deposit do I need?

Most lenders start at 5% of the property value, though 10%+ unlocks noticeably better rates. For buy-to-let, expect 20–25%. We'll tell you exactly where you stand in your free consultation.

Can I get a mortgage if I'm self-employed?

Yes. You'll typically need 1–2 years of accounts or tax returns, and different lenders treat retained profits, day rates and dividends very differently. This is exactly where a whole-of-market broker earns their keep.

Should I fix my rate, and for how long?

It depends on your plans and appetite for risk. There's no universal answer. We'll walk you through fixed vs tracker and 2 vs 5-year terms in the context of your situation, not a sales target.

My bank already offered me a rate. Can you beat it?

Often, yes. Your bank only shows you its own products. Even when we can't beat it, we'll tell you straight, and you'll know your deal was checked against the whole market.
