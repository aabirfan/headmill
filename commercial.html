Commercial questions

Good to know

How fast can a bridging loan complete?

In genuinely urgent cases, bridging can complete in 1–2 weeks, but that relies on responsive solicitors, a straightforward valuation and clean paperwork. A more realistic norm is 2–4 weeks. Tell us your deadline at the start and we'll only approach lenders who can actually hit it.

What can I use a bridging loan for?

Common uses include auction purchases, breaking a chain, buying a property that isn't yet mortgageable, funding refurbishment before refinancing, or releasing capital quickly from property you own. What matters most to lenders is a credible exit, usually a sale or a refinance onto longer-term borrowing.

Do you arrange finance for limited companies and SPVs?

Yes. Much of the commercial and bridging lending we arrange is to limited companies and special purpose vehicles, and many specialist lenders prefer it. We'll work with your accountant or solicitor where needed, though we don't give tax advice on which structure suits you.

What deposit does a commercial mortgage need?

Typically 25–40% of the property value, depending on whether it's owner-occupied or investment, the sector, and the strength of the business or tenant covenant. Stronger trading figures and established tenants generally unlock the lower end of that range.
